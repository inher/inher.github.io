_Biodiversity Talk Series_

### Bird Behaviour: Decoding Courtship, Cooperation and Cleverness in the wild
+ date : *Tue 2nd March 26*
+ time :   *6PM*
+ speaker :  *Dr. Parvish Pandya* (Retd. Vice-principal, Bhavan's College, Mumbai; Consultant-Directory, Sanctuary Nature Foundation and Governing Council member, BNHS)

Join Dr. Parvish Pandya for an engaging talk on bird behaviour, exploring how birds communicate, form social bonds, and solve problems in the wild. The session will cover courtship displays and mating strategies, cooperative behaviours such as group foraging and nesting, and examples of avian intelligence and problem-solving. Dr. Pandya will discuss field observation techniques, notable case studies, and the conservation implications of understanding behavioural ecology. There will be time for questions and discussion at the end.
